Checked in your browser only. Nothing you type is sent or saved.

    What makes a password strong?

    • Length beats complexity. Aim for 14 or more characters.
    • Never reuse passwords. If one site is breached, attackers try the same password everywhere.
    • Avoid personal details. Names, birthdays and “Qona” or “sacco” are easy to guess.
    • Use a password manager and turn on two-factor authentication where offered.

    How to spot a fake email

    Six warning signs to check before you click anything

    • 📧

      Check the sender

      Look at the real address, not just the display name.

    • ⏰

      Urgency and threats

      "Act now or lose access." Attackers rush you.

    • 👋

      Generic greeting

      Real messages usually use your name and details.

    • 🔗

      Hover before you click

      Hover over a link to see where it really goes.

    • 📎

      Unexpected attachments

      Be careful with unexpected invoices and .exe files.

    • 🔑

      Asks for secrets or money

      No genuine IT desk asks for your password.

    Not sure? Don't click. Contact the sender another way, then report it.
